UPCOMING HACKATHONS IN EUROPE
=============================

1. CASSINI Hackathons
   Description: Pan-European competitions focused on EU space data (Galileo, Copernicus) held simultaneously across multiple countries.
   Type: In-person / Distributed across Europe
   Focus: Space data, Earth observation

2. EUDIS Hackathon (EU Defence Innovation Scheme)
   Description: EU Defence Innovation Scheme events focusing on defense and space applications.
   Type: In-person / Virtual
   Focus: Defense technology, Space applications

3. Pan-European Quantum Internet Hackathon
   Description: Organized by the Quantum Internet Alliance across nodes in cities like Amsterdam, Dresden, Paris, and Poznań.
   Type: In-person / Distributed
   Focus: Quantum computing, Quantum internet

4. BioHackathon Europe
   Description: Annual gathering hosted by ELIXIR focused on open science and bioinformatics.
   Type: In-person / Annual event
   Focus: Bioinformatics, Open science, Life sciences

5. European Big Data Hackathon 2025
   Description: Foster expertise in integrating Earth Observation data and big data analytics.
   Type: In-person / Distributed
   Focus: Big data, Earth observation, Data science
